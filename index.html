Categories

Rules where it's not obvious

  1. Label by the kind of business that was paid, not the occasion. Groceries bought on holiday are groceries.
  2. A refund or a payout takes the category of what it reverses. A returned parcel is spend.shopping and a casino win is spend.gambling.
  3. A move between the customer's own accounts is labelled on both rows. If the money goes into or out of an account kept for saving, it's transfer.savings, even when the bank calls that account a checking account.
  4. Paying the credit card bill is credit.loan, and so are mortgage payments (bolån, hypotek). The card bill arriving on the card account is transfer.own.
  5. Paying a Klarna, Qliro or Walley invoice is credit.loan. A card purchase that runs through a payment provider (KLARNA*, ZETTLE_*, SUMUP *, IZ *, PAYPAL *) takes the category of the shop behind it.
  6. Money to or from a person takes the category its message names ("hyra" is rent, so spend.housing). With no message, it's transfer.person.
